About

Sharing our journeys

We’re retired and spending more time on the road—and in the air— collecting memories instead of meetings. This little site is our scrapbook: pictures and notes from the places we’ve loved.

We’ve started with Québec and Ontario in 2016—visiting Aunt Mary Jo and Uncle Jack in Ottawa, with biking, boating, hiking, and the scenery of Eastern Canada—then Iceland in October 2018, Amsterdam in June 2023, Switzerland in July 2023, and Prague and Vienna in March 2026. More journeys can join the scrapbook as we dig through the albums.
