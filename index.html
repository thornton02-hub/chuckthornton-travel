About

Sharing our journeys

We’re retired and spending more time on the road—and in the air— collecting memories instead of meetings. This little site is our scrapbook: pictures, short videos, and notes from the places we’ve loved.

We’ve started with Iceland in October 2018—coastal walks, rainbows after the rain, and quiet churches under big skies—and Amsterdam in June 2023: canals, houseboats, and a stop at the Anne Frank House. More journeys can join the scrapbook as we dig through the albums.
